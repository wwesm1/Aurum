The Making of a Masterpiece

Four centuries of obsession,
distilled into a single second.

Every AURUM timepiece is the result of an uncompromising pursuit — where raw titanium, sapphire and gold are shaped by hand into an instrument that measures not just time, but legacy.

01 — Design

Conceived in shadow, revealed in gold

Each silhouette begins as a single sketch, refined over 400 hours until proportion, weight and light converge into something inevitable.

02 — Movement

The beating heart, assembled in silence

312 components. 47 jewels. A perpetual motion engineered to deviate by less than two seconds across five days — a tolerance only the patient can achieve.

03 — Materials

Grade-5 titanium, sapphire, 18k gold

We source a single sapphire crystal per case — grown over 16 days, polished to optical clarity, harder than steel and more transparent than glass.

04 — Craftsmanship

Finished by hand, signed by its maker

A single master watchmaker assembles, regulates and seals each timepiece, then engraves their initials beneath the balance wheel — a covenant of accountability.
